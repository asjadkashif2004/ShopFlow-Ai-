# ShopPilot: 2-minute demo script

Use Chrome or Edge (voice input needs them). Keep the PayPal Sandbox **buyer** login handy.

## Before you start

- Open the app once a few minutes before, so the backend, Groq and Supabase are awake.
- Run the headphones mission once to warm the cache, then reload the page.
- Have the Orders page empty or with one earlier order, so the new one stands out.
- Record a backup video of this exact flow in case the venue Wi-Fi fails.

## The flow

**0:00 – 0:15 · The problem**
> "Shopping online means twenty tabs and an hour of comparing specs. ShopPilot does that research for you, shows its work, and only buys when you say so."

**0:15 – 0:40 · Ask (use the mic)**
Click the mic and say: *"Wireless headphones under a hundred dollars with noise cancelling and good battery life."*
While Mission Control runs, narrate:
> "Five agents are working. The Intent Agent on Groq understands the request, the Search Agent queries the catalog in Supabase, the Budget Agent checks prices, and the Comparison Agent scores every option."

**0:40 – 1:05 · Show the work (this is the differentiator)**
Open the agents one by one:
- **Intent Agent:** "This is the structured JSON from Groq. It can only use specs that exist in our catalog."
- **Search Agent:** "This is the real SQL it ran on Supabase."
- **Comparison Agent:** "Transparent scores: value, rating, fit and specs."
- **Explainer:** "These are the only facts the AI was allowed to use. Any price it mentions that isn't here gets rejected, so it can't make numbers up."

**1:05 – 1:20 · Refine in one click**
Point at the amber "just over budget" card, then click **Raise budget to $120**.
> "If I stretch my budget by twenty dollars, the agents re-run and the higher-rated Hushwave Studio Pro takes the top spot."

**1:20 – 1:50 · Pay with PayPal**
Click **Buy with PayPal** and point at the checks:
> "Within budget, price checked again on the server, and ShopPilot never pays on its own."

Click the PayPal button, log in with the sandbox buyer and approve. Back in the app, the receipt shows the PayPal order and transaction IDs.

**1:50 – 2:00 · Close**
Open **Orders**:
> "The payment was created and captured through PayPal's Server SDK, generated by APIMatic, and saved in Supabase. The AI does the research; you stay in control of the purchase."

## If something goes wrong

- **Groq is slow or rate limited:** ShopPilot switches to a backup model, then to rules, and keeps working. The engine tag on the timeline shows which one ran.
- **PayPal popup blocked:** allow popups for the site, or open the app's direct URL instead of an embedded frame.
- **No internet at all:** stop the backend and the UI runs the full demo in the browser.
